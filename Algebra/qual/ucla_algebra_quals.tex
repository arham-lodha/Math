This document collects all extractable problems from UCLA algebra qualifying examinations from Spring 2000 through Spring 2026. The exams from 2000--2011 were digitized via OCR and are included here; their mathematical notation may contain minor OCR artifacts.

Problems are organized into eight chapters by topic. Within each chapter, problems are sorted by difficulty:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\bigstar$ --- Standard: direct application of definitions or classical theorems.
  \item $\bigstar\bigstar$ --- Moderate: requires combining multiple ideas or moderate cleverness.
  \item $\bigstar\bigstar\bigstar$ --- Hard: requires significant insight, unusual constructions, or advanced techniques.
\end{itemize}

Within the same difficulty level, problems appear in chronological order. An index at the end lists all problems organized by exam.
